01 / Why us

Four things that go wrong. Handled.

Every experienced HR manager has been burned by at least one of these. Here is how each is closed off.

Problem 01

"The welder cannot weld."

Practical trade testing before any offer, scored against your acceptance criteria and witnessed on video by your engineer if you want it. No paper-only screening.

Problem 02

"Nobody told us it slipped."

A per-worker status sheet from shortlist to arrival. Medical failure, quota change or attestation delay is flagged the day it happens with a proposed fix.

Problem 03

"Two hundred men, eighty beds."

We commit to an arrival curve, not just a headcount — phased against your accommodation, induction capacity and construction programme.

Problem 04

"Half of them left by month three."

Realistic wage and condition briefing before they sign, check-ins at 30 and 90 days, and replacement cover if a worker does not complete probation.

02 / How it runs

Demand letter to arrival gate.

Typical elapsed time for a Gulf deployment is 30–55 days end to end, subject to visa quota and medical slot availability.

01

Requirement mapping

Job description, headcount, salary band, contract length, accommodation and mobilisation date. You get a written note of what is realistic — including where supply actually is this quarter.

02

Sourcing & screening

Our database plus sourcing networks across six states. Documents verified, experience cross-checked, and trade claims taken as claims until tested.

7–10 days

Practical testing at our Jalandhar centre or a third-party facility you nominate. Video or in-person panels, scheduled around your site hours.

3–5 days

10–18 days

03 / What is included

One licence. One invoice.

Sourcing, testing, documentation, emigration clearance and mobilisation stay in-house. Nothing is quietly subcontracted, and there is one accountable name on the file.

  • Category-wise costing agreed before mobilisation starts
  • Practical trade testing to your acceptance criteria
  • Full documentation and eMigrate filing under our own licence
  • Per-worker status tracking through to arrival
  • Pre-departure briefing on wages, hours and conditions
  • 30 and 90 day check-ins, plus replacement cover in probation

What we will not do

We will not promise a trade we cannot source at your wage band, and we will not send untested workers to make a headcount. If the number is not achievable, you will hear it in the first call.
