03 / The house

Started in Dubai. Built in Punjab.

Sagar International Manpower was founded in 1990 in Dubai by Mr. Ram Awadh, supplying labour to contractors who needed people who would actually turn up on day one. The India operation followed under Mr. Pardeep Singh, and Jalandhar has been the recruiting engine ever since.

Three decades later the promise has not moved: clients are not an audience, they are partners — and in their growth lies ours.

06 / The mobilisation

Requirement to runway in six moves.

Typical elapsed time for a Gulf deployment is 30–55 days from signed demand letter to arrival, depending on visa quota and medical slots.

Scroll sideways →

01

Requirement mapping

Job description, headcount, salary band, contract length and mobilisation date. We confirm in writing what is realistic — including where the supply actually is this quarter.

48 hours

02

Sourcing & screening

Our own database plus recruiting networks in Punjab, Haryana, UP, Bihar, Odisha and Tamil Nadu. Every CV is checked against the trade, not the job title on it.

7–10 days

03

Trade test & interview

Practical testing at our Jalandhar centre or a third-party facility you nominate. Video or in-person panels scheduled around your site hours, not ours.

3–5 days

04

Medical & documentation

GAMCA / Wafid medical, police clearance, attestation, contract signing and ECR handling — each worker tracked on a status sheet you can see.

10–18 days

05

Visa & emigration clearance

Stamping and eMigrate clearance filed under Licence B-1550. Delays get flagged the day they happen, not at the weekly call.

7–14 days

06

Mobilisation & aftercare

Ticketing, pre-departure briefing, airport handover, arrival confirmation — then a check-in at 30 and 90 days, and replacement cover inside probation.

Ongoing
