01 · Manpower consultancy

01 / Manpower Consultancy

Volume hiring you can build a programme around

From a five-man specialist team to a large mobilisation phased across a construction programme. We take the demand letter, map it against real supply, and commit to dates in writing.

Most agencies quote a headcount. We quote a headcount and a curve — how many land in week four, week eight, week twelve — because a site that receives 200 men at once and has beds for 80 has a different problem than the one it started with.

  • Written manpower plan with a phased arrival curve
  • Category-wise costing before you commit
  • Per-worker status sheet from shortlist to arrival

02 / Recruitment Consultant

One named consultant, start to finish

Every requirement is handled by one dedicated consultant — the same person who takes your first call runs the sourcing, sits in on the trade test, and chases the file through documentation. Nothing changes hands between departments you never meet.

Candidates are tested practically before they are offered to you. Welders weld to the procedure you specify. Carpenters set shuttering. Drivers drive. Your engineer can sit on the panel by video if you want to see it happen.

Practical trade test

Witnessed, recorded, and scored against the client's own acceptance criteria — not a generic pass mark.

Client interview panel

In-person visits arranged and hosted, or video panels scheduled around your site hours rather than ours.

03 / Manpower Services

The part where most deployments actually fail

A worker who clears the trade test and then loses six weeks to a rejected medical or an attestation error costs you the same as one who never existed. Documentation, medical, visa and mobilisation are handled in-house, under our recruiting agent licence, as a tracked pipeline rather than a pile of files.

  • Medical scheduling and re-test management
  • Police clearance certificates and document attestation
  • Employment contract preparation and signing under Emigration Act rules
  • Emigration clearance and visa stamping coordination
  • Ticketing, departure scheduling and arrival confirmation
  • Support line kept open after the worker lands

Delays get flagged the day they happen

If a medical fails or a quota moves, you hear it that day with a proposed fix — not on the weekly call after the date has already slipped.

04 · Contract staffing services

04 / Contract Staffing Services

Crews sized to the contract, not to a headcount plan

Shutdowns, turnarounds, seasonal peaks and single-project builds need a different instrument than permanent hiring. We assemble fixed-term crews with contracts, insurance and repatriation planned against the actual end date.

A

Shutdown & turnaround

Welders, fitters, riggers and scaffolders mobilised for a defined window and demobilised cleanly.

B

Seasonal contracts

Farm and support-services crews for European seasonal permits, with return-year continuity.

C

Project builds

Full trade mix for a single project, phased to the construction programme and released at handover.

05 / IT Staffing Services

White-collar hiring, recruited on evidence

Software, support and infrastructure engineers, plus the wider technical and back-office roles that sit alongside them — QA/QC, planners, draughtsmen, accountants, HR coordinators. Screened on capability and reference checks rather than a keyword match against a job spec.

IT staffing

Software, support and infrastructure engineers on permanent, contract or offshore-extension models.

Technical & professional

Back-office and technical roles hired on the same evidence-first standard as our trade placements.

06 / Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO)

We hold the funnel. You hold the decision.

For employers hiring continuously rather than once, we run as an embedded recruitment function — sourcing, screening and shortlisting against your live requisitions, on your timeline, reporting into whoever owns headcount on your side.

  • A managed, always-open sourcing funnel instead of a one-off search
  • Shortlists scored against your acceptance criteria, not ours
  • Worker check-ins after placement, with replacement cover in probation
  • One point of contact across every requisition, not a new recruiter each time
