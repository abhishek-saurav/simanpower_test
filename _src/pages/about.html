01 / Origin

Dubai, 1990.

Mr. Ram Awadh founded Sagar International Manpower in Dubai in 1990, at the point when Gulf contractors were building faster than they could staff. The gap was not bodies — it was reliability. Crews arrived late, papers were wrong, and half of a shipment would not last the probation period.

The company was built to close that gap: test people properly before offering them, do the documentation once and do it right, and stand behind the deployment after the plane lands.

The India operation followed under Mr. Pardeep Singh, and Jalandhar — sitting in the middle of Punjab's skilled-trade belt — became the recruiting engine. Thirty-five years on, that is still where the work happens.

03 / How we work

Four rules we do not bend.

They are unglamorous. They are also the entire reason clients come back after a decade.

01 — Honesty

We will tell you when we cannot do it

If the trade is not available at that wage in that quarter, you hear it in the first call — not after six weeks of silence and a half-filled demand letter.

02 — Testing

Nobody is offered untested

Trade tests are practical and witnessed. A CV is a claim; a weld, a cut or a driving assessment is evidence.

03 — Paperwork

One licence, one chain of custody

Documentation, emigration clearance and visa stay in-house under our own registration. Nothing is quietly handed to a sub-agent.

04 — Aftercare

The job is not done at the airport

Arrival confirmation, check-ins at 30 and 90 days, a support line in three languages, and replacement cover inside probation.
