Before you pay anyone, anywhere — read this

Any service charge is collected only against an official receipt in the company's name, within the limits set under the Emigration Act. Never hand cash to an individual, a sub-agent or a "friend of the office". Never surrender your original passport to anyone who will not give you a receipt for it. And always verify a recruiting agent's registration on emigrate.gov.in — ours is B-1550/PUN/PART/1000+/5/9486/2019, eMigrate ID RA5466131.

03 / Get ready

What to bring on day one.

Having these ready can take two to three weeks off your timeline. Bring originals plus one photocopy set.

  • Passport valid for at least 18 months, with clear pages
  • ITI, NCVT, diploma or trade certificates
  • Experience and relieving letters from previous employers
  • Driving licence, if applying for a driving role
  • Recent passport-size photographs, white background
  • Aadhaar and PAN
  • Any previous visa or emigration clearance stamps
Pre-departure briefing · Jalandhar

Warning signs of a fake offer

A visa "guaranteed" without an interview. Payment demanded in cash to a personal account. No written contract before departure. An agency that will not tell you its registration number. A salary far above the market for that trade. If you see any of these, stop and call us — even if the offer is not ours.
